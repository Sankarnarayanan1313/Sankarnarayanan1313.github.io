Privacy Policy

MB Framework · last updated 29 August 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the MB Framework application and its use of Google APIs. MB Framework is a private, single-user system operated by its author for personal equity research. It has no other users and is not offered as a service.

2. Information collected

The application collects no personal information from anyone. It has no sign-up, no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking and no logging of visitors.

The only data it handles is public market information about listed companies — filings, announcements and price history — together with the author's own analysis of it. None of that is personal data about any individual.

3. Google user data accessed

The application requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, authorised once by the account owner, and uses it solely to maintain the account owner's own backups. Specifically it:

  • creates a folder named MB_Framework_Backups in the owner's Drive;
  • uploads compressed backup archives into that folder;
  • lists and deletes the archives it previously uploaded, to enforce a retention limit.

It does not read, index, modify, download or transmit any other file in the Drive, and it does not access any other Google service or Google account. The access is used exclusively to provide the backup feature described on the home page and for no other purpose.

4. How the data is used

Backup archives are written to Google Drive so that the author's research data survives the loss of the server it normally lives on. They are read back only during a restore.

5. Sharing

No data is shared, sold, transferred or disclosed to any third party — not for advertising, not for analytics, not for AI or machine-learning training, and not for any other purpose. There is no third party involved. The only parties are the author's own server and the author's own Google Drive.

6. Storage, security and retention

Archives are stored in the account owner's private Google Drive, visible to no one else, and on the author's own server. The OAuth credential is held in the rclone configuration file on that server, readable only by its operating-system owner.

Archives are rotated automatically on a fixed retention schedule: the daily tier keeps 14 copies, the weekly tier 5, and the monthly tier 3. Older archives are deleted permanently by the application itself.

7. Revoking access, and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends the application's ability to reach the Drive. The stored archives are ordinary files in the owner's Drive and can be deleted at any time from Drive itself.

8. Children

The application is not directed at children and has no users other than its author.
